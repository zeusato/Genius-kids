# Preschool menu artwork

Created in Google Flow (Nano Banana 2, landscape) on 2026-09-16 for Genius Kids.
Project: https://flow.google.com/project/7b20f6a9-d1ff-4fbb-9152-27694219b8d5

| Local file | Flow asset title | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| preschool-alphabet.webp | Fox sitting near alphabet blocks | Fox, wooden A/B/C, apple, bee and book |
| preschool-counting.webp | Rabbit and wooden number blocks | Rabbit, wooden 1/2/3, abacus and apples |
| preschool-colors.webp | Bear and toy garden diorama | Bear, wooden rainbow, palette and shape sorter |

Shared art direction: tactile wood and clay toy dioramas, cream and sage palette,
gentle morning light, preschool-friendly characters, uncluttered landscape scenes.
Prompts and original generated images are retained in the Flow project.
Exported images are encoded as WebP at 1200 px and 400 px using
`scripts/prepare-preschool-art.mjs`. The `-sm` files are the small variants.
Text labels and teaching content remain live HTML in the application.
