# Piano Nhí — audio credits

Acoustic samples: Salamander Grand Piano, Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5).
Redistributed MP3 subset from https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
Original project's README identifies the license as Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source project: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Files: C4, Ds4, Fs4, A4, C5, Ds5, Fs5, A5, C6.mp3.
Downloaded 2026-09-17; MP3 files unmodified. Playback transposes neighboring samples
and applies gain and release envelopes. No endorsement by the sample author is implied.

Electric piano, organ, guitar, wood and music-box sounds are original mathematical
synthesis patches in games/Piano/audio.ts. The guitar sound is a plucked-string model.
