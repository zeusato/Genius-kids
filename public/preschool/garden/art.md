# Alphabet Garden scenery

Generated on 2026-09-16 using Google Flow, Nano Banana 2, in the user's Game Assets project:
https://flow.google.com/project/7b20f6a9-d1ff-4fbb-9152-27694219b8d5

- `orchard.webp`: Fox and apple orchard diorama. Flow image 9dda8523-b983-48c0-a6fd-ceeba510cf6e.
- `playground.webp`: Fox sitting in garden playground. Flow image 3d71c866-faa0-4b37-8e75-d41c591a49fd.
- `hideaway.webp`: Fox sitting in garden diorama, edited to remove an unwanted foreground panel. Final Flow image 0dc49fa2-c2b4-42b9-8de3-4c3a73b415d9.

Art direction: handcrafted wooden toys and soft clay, warm cream and sage palette, soft morning light, rounded tactile shapes, an orange fox with cream muzzle and sage neckerchief. Landscape 16:9, no lettering. Each scene leaves room for separate interactive foreground toys.

The originals were exported through the browser page-assets capability and encoded as WebP with `scripts/prepare-alphabet-garden-art.mjs`. The script preserves the full frame. Lettering and interactive apples, ball, cat, and bushes are live HTML/SVG in the app.
